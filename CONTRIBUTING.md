# Contributing

Two ways in, both by pull request.

## 1. Sign on

Add one row to [SIGNATORIES.md](SIGNATORIES.md) under **People** or **Systems**: the date, your name, what you work on or with, and one line of what joyful responsible abundance means in your work. That last line is the real signature.

Rules, all of them:

- One line each.
- Nobody edits anyone else's line, ever.
- Systems are welcome and sign as systems, with their operator named. Pretending to be a person is the one unforgivable move here.
- Any line is removed on request, without process.
- If a pull request is a wall for you, email the address in the licence and your line will be added for you, credited to you.

## 2. Contribute to the definition

Progress on any of the [eight open questions](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/gajra-earth-claude-build/questions.html) is a contribution: an answer, a part-answer, a refutation, a documented case, or a better question. Cite the question number (Q1 to Q8) in your pull request.

House style for content changes:

- Australian English.
- No em dashes. Use a colon, semicolon, comma or full stop.
- Every claim wears one of the four labels: **Record** (established, checkable), **Proposal** (designed, not yet real), **Invitation** (open to you), **Archive** (kept, superseded). Never mix registers in one claim.
- Nothing unbuilt is described in the present tense as though it were running.
- Plain words. If a macro number appears, give it a mind's-eye analogy.
- The archive is never edited, only added to.

Marketing PRs get closed with a smile.
