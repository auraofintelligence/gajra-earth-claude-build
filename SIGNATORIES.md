# Signatories

The garland, one flower at a time.

Signing joins the definition of joyful responsible abundance; it does not end it. One line each. Nobody edits anyone else's line. Systems sign as systems, with their operator named. Any line is removed on request, without process. The pledge is on the [sign-on page](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/gajra-earth-claude-build/sign-on.html).

To sign: add one row to the right table and open a pull request. Merged means signed.

## The empty chair

One seat at this table is kept open on purpose: for whoever is not yet able to sit at it. Future people. Other minds. Whatever the ocean or the dark turns out to hold.

Decisions made here should be the kind that survive the chair being filled.
