The record

Nothing was deleted. The thread keeps its knots.

A commons that edits its own past cannot ask you to trust it with the future. So here is the past, unedited, with what was learned tied on beside it.

What was planned

Archive

The name and the idea date from 2018. Through 2023 and 2024, working with a large language model, the founder drafted an ambitious architecture: a token, a DAO, and a synchronised three-day world festival planned for July 2025, the fortieth anniversary of Live Aid, themed Past, Present and Future, carrying a world vote on core values for the alignment of artificial general intelligence.

A token sale was planned for the first quarter of 2025. It did not launch. July 2025 passed without the festival. The site advertising both stayed up long after, which was its own failure of the record, and is the specific thing this page exists to correct.

The ICO-era site is preserved exactly as it stood: view it here. Its licence file as shipped is preserved beside it.

What was learned

Four knots, kept visible.

Tokens before trust was the wrong order.

A currency is a claim on a community that does not exist yet. The order has been reversed: trust first, chain later, and only if the signatories ever decide it earns a place.

A destination does not need a currency.

The valuable part of the old design was never the token. It was the question underneath: aligned to what? That part is now the entire project.

One person cannot hold a world event.

Fifty thousand towns was not a delusion of grandeur; the city lists were real and downloaded. It was a plan whose missing input was other people, and no amount of architecture substitutes for them. The signing mechanism is the correction.

The world vote was early, not wrong.

A collective vote on the core values that steer powerful systems remains a good idea. It returns, in humbler clothes, as the signatures file and the definition process: a world vote run at the speed of trust.

In kintsugi, the repaired crack is shown in gold rather than hidden, and the object is considered more itself for having been broken and mended. This page is the gold.
