The record

Nothing was deleted. The thread keeps its knots.

A commons that edits its own past cannot ask you to trust it with the future. So here is the past, unedited, with what was learned tied on beside it.

What was planned

Archive

The name and the idea date from 2018. Through 2023 and 2024, working with a large language model, the founder drafted an ambitious architecture: a token, a DAO, and a synchronised three-day world festival planned for July 2025, the fortieth anniversary of Live Aid, themed Past, Present and Future, carrying a world vote on core values for the alignment of artificial general intelligence.

A token sale was planned for the first quarter of 2025. It did not launch. July 2025 passed without the festival. The site advertising both stayed up long after, which was its own failure of the record, and is the specific thing this page exists to correct.

What is kept here, and what is not. The original gajra.earth was built by hand in WordPress, years before any of these repositories existed, and no copy of it is held here. If you want to see it, the Internet Archive is where to look.

What is kept below is something else: a single-page site that lived in the GAJRA-earth repository, later and much smaller. It is kept unedited because it is part of the record, but calling it the old site would be a tidier story than the true one. Its licence file as shipped is preserved beside it.

The interview

An earlier version of this page listed lessons: tokens before trust was the wrong order, one person cannot hold a world event, and so on. Tidy, and written by somebody who was not there. Verdicts about a person's decisions are cheap when the person is available to ask.

So instead, the questions. They are deliberately uncomfortable, of the kind a sceptical journalist would put, and they stay on this page whether or not the answers flatter anybody. The answers are the founder's own words, unedited, added as he gets to them. An unanswered question stays visible as unanswered.

Anyone can add a question by pull request. Questions are not removed for being awkward. If one is unfair, the answer is the place to say so.
