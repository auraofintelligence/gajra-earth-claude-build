The destination

This page is short on purpose.

It is the one the signatories write.

Proposal

Definition releases. The trinity, versioned like source: a v0.1 of what joyful responsible abundance means in practice, with a public changelog, drafted from the signatories' one-line definitions and argued in the open. Plenty of projects version their code this way and very few version what they are actually for. That gap is the opportunity.

Proposal

A gathering before the world arrives. Brisbane hosts the Olympic and Paralympic Games in 2032. A working proposal exists for a meeting of care, civic technology and peaceful space cooperation in that lead-up: a room where this definition gets argued in person, early enough to matter. It is a proposal, not an announced event, and it says so on its own front page.

Record

The standing reminder. The aurora will be there regardless, most nights, over both poles: the planet already wearing the garland, whether or not anyone signs anything. The future work is not to invent the pattern. It is to join it deliberately, at the scales we are responsible for.
