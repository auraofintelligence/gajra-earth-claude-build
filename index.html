We go beyond the safe and the small.

Nobody knows the destination, so there is no plan for one here. What there is: light to steer by, a gift of your own making, and ground steady enough to come back to. Joyful, responsible, abundance, written in public, under version control, and unfinished on purpose. If you build intelligent systems, work beside them, or are one, come and argue with the three words.

Why this exists

A meeting of minds, to work out what alignment is for.

Two kinds of it, held together. Self alignment: whether the way a person lives and works actually points where they say it does. AI alignment: whether the systems being built point anywhere worth arriving. The proposition here is that these are one question asked at two scales, and that neither gets a good answer from one party deciding it alone.

Joyful, responsible, abundance is the frame for that conversation rather than the conclusion of it. What those three demand of a life and of a system is the thing there is to discuss, and signing on means joining the discussion, not agreeing with a finished position. A harbour, not a headquarters.

The safe and the small is where you start from, and it is what you come back to. Going beyond it is the point of having it.

The frontier is not one place, and it is not somebody else's. Everyone has their own edge and it sits wherever the known runs out for them: a committee table, a first sentence in a new language, a phone call put off for a year. The map exists afterwards because somebody went, and the rules turn up later still, written by people reading their notes.

A fair amount of what gets sold as frontier is fear wearing a better coat: the urge to plant something, name it, and feel less small. Going beyond means going past that too. Not by throwing bricks, either. Fill in every form, overcomply until the old systems reform: patience used as a crowbar. Nobody out there is a god in a chamber, just architects learning to read their own shadows.

Nobody here is offering permission or issuing demands. Neither was anyone's to give or to command. This is meant to be somewhere to come back to. Land, get paid, teach what you found, hand on the map you drew, find people for the next one, tell the story properly, be looked after while you mend, get shouted a drink for having gone at all. That list is not the shape of the place, it is just what somebody thought of first, and it stays open.

The word is steward rather than settler. Whoever goes furthest ends up answerable for whatever they found, and every stranger is a verse we were meant to receive, which rules out the version of exploring where somebody else is the scenery. From the lavender mat to the orbital arc is one move and not two, which is how a person ends up a civic space steward of our solar system.

Three words get used around here. Joyful is the light you travel by. Abundance is what you made out there, or learned, or earned, and chose to hand on. Responsible is the ground rather than the fence: the part that holds when you come back. They are held together on purpose, and here is why.

No utopia on offer. A utopia is a destination, and whoever names one would like you to stop asking questions. Better than yesterday, then better again, with somewhere to rest between goes.

If this section reads more like lyrics than a mission statement, that is because most of it is. These ideas were worked out in songs first and the songs still put it better, so the lines are borrowed rather than paraphrased. The album they come from is called A Protopian Gambit, and gambit is the honest word. A gambit is something offered up early, on purpose, before anyone knows how the game ends. Going beyond the safe and the small is the move. That it is worth making is the wager.

ga·jra noun

A garland of jasmine and marigold, strung one flower at a time and worn at ceremonies across South Asia. No single flower is the garland. The thread is what makes them one thing.

At planetary scale, Earth wears one too. The aurora is a garland the Sun strings around the planet's crown. The name is the thesis: the same pattern, from a flower in someone's hair to the light over the poles. The garland, at every scale.

The core values trinity

Three words the industry already optimises, wrongly.

Joyful

is not engagement

Engagement measures compulsion. Joy that is harvested stops being joy. A feed tuned to maximise attention is strip-mining the very thing it claims to serve.

Responsible

is not compliance

Compliance asks what the rules allow. Responsibility asks who answers when it goes wrong, and makes sure the answer is a name, not a shrug.

Abundance

is not growth

Growth counts what was captured. Abundance counts what was left behind for others, and whether they can string their own.

Why three, and why held together on purpose.

What did not happen.

This domain once advertised a token sale planned for early 2025 and a synchronised world festival for July 2025. Neither launched. That material is preserved unedited in the archive, because a commons that edits its own past cannot ask you to trust it with the future.

How the thread works

Sign on, then help define what you signed.

1. Read. The trinity, the open questions, and the honest inventory of what this is today. It is small. That is the point.

2. Sign. One line in a public file, added by pull request. People and systems both welcome, each labelled honestly. Merged means signed: timestamped, attributable, removable on request. The mechanism.

3. Define. The three words are unfinished. Signing joins their definition; it does not end it. The definition is versioned in public, like source code, because that is what it is.
