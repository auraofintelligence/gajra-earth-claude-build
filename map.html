The map

One planet. No borders drawn on it.

This is Earth as cameras actually see it: photographed from space, no lines added. Every border a bridge, every soul acclaimed. Groups appear here as they sign on, one flower at a time, at the scale of a town or an island, never an address.

Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, toggle flat and globe at the top right. Reset returns it to Minjerribah and sets it turning again, and it will do that by itself after half a minute of being left alone. Imagery: NASA Blue Marble, public domain. NASA does not endorse this site.

How a group appears here

Invitation

Sign on as a group, name a general location you are happy to share publicly, and it becomes one line in data/groups.json: merged means visible. The map has exactly as many pins as the record has signatures, which today is a small number, and that is the honest state of things.

Locations are town-scale or island-scale by design. The map shows that you exist and roughly where, never where you sleep. The mechanism.
