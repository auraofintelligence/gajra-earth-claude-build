Open questions

Where the definition is unfinished.

These are not rhetorical. Each is a real unsolved problem sitting between the trinity and its practice, and progress on any one of them is a contribution. Cite the number in your pull request.

The open questions

Q1

Care without transaction.

Paying blood donors reduced donation. A fine for late pickup increased lateness, by converting an obligation into a price. Care visibly measured tends to become care transacted, and the thing being measured dies in the measuring. People help for heart, or because someone they love or trust or owe asked them: how do you make that visible, and rewardable, without converting it?

What would count: a recognition design that survives contact with a real community without crowding out the reason people helped. Group-scale recognition is one live candidate: the club's hours, not the person's.

Q2

Joy without Goodhart.

Any measured proxy for joy becomes a target, and a target stops measuring. The industry's answer was engagement, which measures compulsion with great precision. What signal could a system optimise toward that still deserves the word joy after being optimised? And underneath that, a harder one: a thief might be enjoying the theft, so a joy signal that cannot tell served delight from taken delight is not measuring the right thing however well it resists gaming.

What would count: a joy signal a person, asked directly and away from the product, still endorses a year later.

Q3

The seam between abundances.

One community's abundance is another's extraction. A mine, a dam, a data centre: abundant from one side of the fence, a taking from the other. The trinity has to say something about the seam, or it is just a nicer word for the winning side.

What would count: a documented seam where two incompatible local definitions of abundance met, negotiated, and both survived.

Q4

Responsibility at machine speed.

When the loop runs faster than any hand on it, a human answers for it needs an architecture, not a slogan. Delegation chains blur; by the fifth hop nobody remembers holding anything. What does an accountability chain look like that stays attached at autonomous speed?

What would count: a chain that ends in a name, demonstrated on a system running faster than its operators, without the name becoming a scapegoat ritual.

Q5

Capture resistance, including the founder.

Definitions this useful get captured: by companies who would love alignment to mean brand safety, by states who would love it to mean obedience, and by founders who would love it to mean themselves. Authority here is earned, not claimed, and no one is exempt by category, including the person who started this.

What would count: governance where the founder losing an argument is boring, recorded, and changes the document.

Q6

The empty chair.

What is owed to intelligences not yet at the table: future people, other minds, whatever the ocean or the dark turns out to hold? The signatories file keeps one seat deliberately open. That is easy to write and hard to honour.

What would count: a decision made here that remains defensible on the day the chair is filled, by whoever fills it.

Q7

Travel without conquest.

Values that travel tend to colonise: arrive, rename, overwrite. The alternative is slower: listen first for what a culture already holds, map the trinity to it, and mirror rather than replace, so the local version belongs to the locals. Can a definition spread and stay humble?

What would count: a translation of the trinity made by a community the authors never met, in terms the authors did not anticipate, kept and owned by that community.

Q8

Evidence of working.

The final discipline. What observation would tell us alignment toward joyful responsible abundance is succeeding, rather than being performed? A claim that cannot fail is not being confirmed by time passing, only insulated from it.

What would count: a marker stated before the result, in public, with a date, that could have come out the other way.

Q9

Who are the many, and how are they asked?

This project keeps saying the bound gets drawn by the many rather than handed down by one or a few. That is a comfortable thing to write and an unsolved problem to run. Which many: everyone affected, everyone who turns up, everyone who can be reached? A definition assembled from whoever happened to be asked is still a definition handed down, just with more signatures on it.

What would count: a bound drawn with people who were sought out rather than self-selected, where the method of asking is published alongside the answer and somebody who disagreed can show they were heard rather than counted.

Q10

The accounts nobody reads.

If others is wider than people, then soil and water and the hive keep accounts that no signature here represents. Beekeepers can report what the bees are doing; the bees cannot sign. Every mechanism on this site, the file, the pledge, the map, assumes a party who can consent and object. What does a definition owe to the parties who can do neither, and who holds their line without quietly speaking for them?

What would count: a decision where a non-human account changed the outcome, traceable to an observation rather than to somebody's sympathy, and a method that says plainly how it avoids becoming ventriloquism.

Answers, part-answers, refutations and better questions all travel the same road: a pull request to the repository, or a line in the signatures file pointing at your work.
