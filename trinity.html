The core values trinity

Three words, held together on purpose.

Any two are easy. It is the third that makes it hard, and the hardness is the point. Each pair, without its third, is a familiar failure with a long history.

Joyful

What it names. Delight as an outcome the system is answerable to. Not a mood on a dashboard: the thing a person reports when you ask them directly, away from the product, a year later.

What it rules out. Engagement farming. Dark patterns. Retention shaped like addiction. Any design where the delight is being extracted rather than served. Joy that is harvested stops being joy. That is not a slogan; it is one of the most replicated findings about human motivation.

Still open. Whether joy can be measured at all without the measure becoming a target and eating the thing it measured. That is question two, and it is genuinely unsolved.

Responsible

What it names. A hand that stays on the thing, and a name that answers for it. Attributable, auditable, revocable. The connection is worn like clothing, never grafted like skin: it can always be taken off, by the person, at any moment.

What it rules out. Compliance theatre. Accountability that dissolves into an org chart. Systems whose failures are everyone's fault and therefore no one's. Consent captured once and never asked again.

Still open. What a hand on the thing means when the loop runs faster than any hand. That is question four.

Abundant

What it names. Work measured by what it leaves behind for others, and whether they can build their own with it. Abundance is a property of the wake, not the wallet.

What it rules out. Growth that eats its host. Plenty hoarded into scarcity. Extraction wearing the word abundance as a costume, which is most of what gets sold under that word.

Still open. What happens at the seam where two communities define abundance incompatibly. That is question three.

Why three

Each pair, without its third, is a named failure.

The carnival that eats its host.

Joy plus abundance, but no responsibility. Plenty and delight with no name attached to the consequences. Every extraction boom in history threw a good party first.

Noble scarcity.

Joy plus responsibility, but no abundance. Careful, kind, and running on fumes. It burns out its best people and calls the burnout virtue.

The well-run warehouse.

Responsibility plus abundance, but no joy. Everything accounted for, nothing worth accounting for. Systems this shape are safe right up until everyone quietly leaves.

The trinity is not decoration. It is a three-legged stool, and the failure modes above are what each pair does the moment its third leg is removed. An aligned system is one that can be audited against all three at once.

Signing is joining the definition, not agreeing to a finished one. The three words are deliberately unfinished, and the signatories are the people finishing them.
