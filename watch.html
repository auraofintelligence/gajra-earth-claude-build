The watch

What moved, and where.

A scan runs every morning and writes what it found here: governance, law, data centres, and the alignment conversation, from the bodies that actually set rules. Newest at the top. Every entry carries a source you can go and check, and nothing that scrolls off is deleted.

How to read this

Leads, not verdicts.

Each line is a pointer to something published elsewhere. Follow them up before you rely on them, and the link is there so you can form your own view rather than take this page's.

Each entry carries the date the thing happened, not the date it was noticed. Items from the big powers and the large regulators usually land within a day or two. Items from the African Union, the Pacific Islands Forum, CARICOM, the Arab States and much of Latin America surface in English days later, or only on their own sites, or only in another language, so the scan looks back about a week for those and reports them when it finds them. The alternative is a page that reads fast and shows you Washington and Brussels.

Defence and military items are left out on purpose. Not because they do not matter, but because this project does not attempt to bridge into that domain and civilian voices are routinely ignored there anyway. If something looked important and got excluded on that rule, the day's entry says so.
