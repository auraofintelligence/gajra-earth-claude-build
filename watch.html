The watch

What moved yesterday.

A scan runs every morning and writes what it found here: governance, law, data centres, and the alignment conversation, from the bodies that actually set rules. Newest at the top. Every entry carries a source you can go and check.

How to read this

Leads, not verdicts.

Each line is a pointer to something published elsewhere, written down the morning it appeared. They are leads worth following rather than facts to quote, and the link is there so you can form your own view rather than take this page's.

Defence and military items are left out on purpose. Not because they do not matter, but because this project does not attempt to bridge into that domain and civilian voices are routinely ignored there anyway. If something looked important and got excluded on that rule, the day's entry says so.
